Our Story

How It All Began

HomeOwner Foundation was born from a deep conviction that every Nigerian deserves a safe roof over their head. Founded in 2018, our journey began in the bustling streets of Lagos, where thousands of families lived without shelter or security.

What started as a small community initiative to build five homes for displaced families has grown into a nationwide movement. Today, we operate across 15 states, partnering with local governments, corporate sponsors, and international organizations to address Nigeria's acute housing deficit.

Our approach goes beyond building structures — we create communities. Each project includes access to clean water, schools, healthcare facilities, and vocational training centres, ensuring that families don't just have a home but have the tools to thrive.

Our Mission

To eradicate homelessness in Nigeria by providing safe, dignified, and affordable housing for vulnerable families, while empowering communities through sustainable development programmes.

Our Vision

A Nigeria where no family is without a home — where every citizen has access to safe shelter, clean water, and the opportunity to build a better future for their children and generations to come.
