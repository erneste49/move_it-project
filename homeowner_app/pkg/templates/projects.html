Lagos State

Sunshine Estate, Lagos

Sunshine Estate is a 50-unit housing development located in the Ikorodu area of Lagos, designed to provide affordable, quality homes for low-income families who were previously displaced or living in overcrowded informal settlements.

The estate features well-planned two and three-bedroom bungalows, each fitted with modern amenities, running water, and reliable electricity powered by a combination of grid and solar energy. The community also boasts a children's playground, a multi-purpose community hall, and a primary healthcare centre.

FCT Abuja

Hope Village, Abuja

Our flagship project in the Federal Capital Territory, Hope Village is a 75-unit community designed specifically for families transitioning out of homelessness. Located in the Gwagwalada area, the estate was developed in partnership with the FCT Administration and international donor organisations.

Beyond housing, Hope Village integrates a primary school, a vocational training centre offering courses in tailoring, carpentry, and IT skills, and a community health clinic staffed by volunteer medical professionals. It represents our vision of holistic community development.

Enugu State

Unity Gardens, Enugu

Unity Gardens is a 40-unit eco-friendly development in Enugu State, built almost entirely with locally sourced materials to reduce costs and support the local economy. The project was born from a partnership with the Enugu State Housing Authority and employed over 120 local artisans during construction.

Each home features a kitchen garden and the estate shares a communal farm, enabling families to grow their own food and generate supplementary income. Solar-powered street lighting and a borehole water system serve the entire community.

Kano State

New Dawn Estate, Kano

Addressing the acute housing shortage in Northern Nigeria, New Dawn Estate is an ambitious 60-unit project in Kano State. Currently in Phase 2 of construction, the estate will serve internally displaced families and low-income households in the Kano metropolitan area.

The development features community gardens, a vocational training centre focused on agriculture and small-scale manufacturing, a mosque, a church, and safe play areas for children. Phase 1 with 25 completed units has already been handed over to beneficiaries.

Rivers State

Harmony Homes, Port Harcourt

Harmony Homes is a 45-unit waterfront community in Port Harcourt, specifically designed for families displaced by the demolition of informal waterfront settlements in Rivers State. The project is a collaboration between HomeOwner Foundation, the Rivers State Government, and UNDP Nigeria.

The estate incorporates flood-resistant construction techniques suited to the Niger Delta terrain, with elevated foundations and proper drainage systems. A fish processing centre and a skills acquisition centre will provide livelihood opportunities for residents.

Oyo State

Heritage Court, Ibadan

Heritage Court is our newest project, currently in the planning and land acquisition phase in Ibadan, Oyo State. Designed to accommodate 80 families, it will be our largest single project to date and marks our expansion into South-West Nigeria beyond Lagos.

The master plan includes a mixed-use development with residential units, a marketplace, a primary school, and recreational spaces. Heritage Court will pilot our new modular construction approach, which promises to reduce build times by 40% and cut costs significantly.
