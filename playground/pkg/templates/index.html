Welcome Message

From the Managing Director

On behalf of the entire HomeOwner Foundation team, I warmly welcome you. Our mission began with a simple yet powerful belief — that every Nigerian, regardless of their circumstances, deserves a safe place to call home.

Over the years, we have witnessed the transformative power of a roof over one's head. A home is not just four walls; it is security, dignity, and the foundation upon which families build their futures. From Lagos to Abuja, from Kano to Port Harcourt, we are building communities one home at a time.

We invite you to join us in this mission. Whether through donations, volunteering, or simply spreading the word, together we can restore hope to thousands of homeless families across Nigeria.

— Emeka Okonkwo Managing Director, HomeOwner Foundation

Our Latest Projects

Explore the communities we're building across Nigeria, bringing safe and affordable homes to families in need.

Sunshine Estate, Lagos

A 50-unit housing development providing affordable homes for low-income families in the heart of Lagos. Complete with community facilities, clean water, and solar power systems for sustainable living.

Hope Village, Abuja

Our flagship project in the Federal Capital Territory, Hope Village features 75 modern bungalows designed for families transitioning out of homelessness, with integrated schools and healthcare centres.
